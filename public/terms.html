5. Session IDs and your data

  • A session ID can be used once and expires 5 minutes after it is made.
  • The linked session is stored encrypted until the ID is used or expires, then it is removed from the site.
  • Your phone number is used only to request a pairing code and is not kept. A hashed fingerprint of it is held briefly to limit repeated attempts.
  • The site has no tracking or advertising cookies. Your device stores only small preferences (theme, sound, music) and, while you are mid-attempt, a temporary note so a page refresh can continue it.
  • Once the bot has your session, how it stores and uses it is covered by what the bot does inside your groups. You can unlink it any time in WhatsApp under Linked devices.
