About TurboFex Pair

TurboFex Pair is the official linking page for the TurboFex WhatsApp group-management bot.

What it does

TurboFex is a bot that helps people run their WhatsApp groups. This website is where you link your own WhatsApp account to the bot. You choose a pairing code or a QR code, confirm it inside WhatsApp, and you receive a one-time session ID that you give to the bot.

How linking works

  1. Choose a method. Enter your number for a pairing code, or scan a QR code.
  2. Confirm in WhatsApp. Open WhatsApp, go to Linked devices and approve the link.
  3. Get your session ID. It looks like TFX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX and is also sent to your WhatsApp chat.
  4. Give it to the bot. The bot uses it once to connect to your account.

How your data is handled

  • A session ID works one time only and expires after 5 minutes.
  • The session behind it is stored encrypted and is removed once the ID has been used or has expired.
  • Your phone number is used only to ask WhatsApp for a pairing code. It is not saved; for rate limiting we keep only a scrambled (hashed) fingerprint of it for a short time.
  • The site sets no tracking or advertising cookies. Your device remembers only small preferences, such as light or dark mode and sound.
