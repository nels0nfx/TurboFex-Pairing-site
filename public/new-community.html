Ways to take part

  • Use and rate plugins. Sign in with GitHub on the Marketplace to rate a plugin or report a problem.
  • Build a plugin. Read the Plugin guide, test it in the Playground, then send it from the Developer Centre.
  • Ask and answer. Share what worked for you in the Telegram community. Someone else probably has the same question.
  • Tell us what to build next. Ideas are welcome in either community.

House rules

  • Be kind and keep it on topic. No spam and no advertising.
  • Never share your session ID with anyone, not even people who say they are helping. Anyone who has it before it is used can connect to your account.
  • Do not ask for or share other people’s WhatsApp accounts.
  • Report a harmful plugin from its page on the Marketplace so a reviewer can look at it.
