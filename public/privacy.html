Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain words, what TurboFex Pair collects when you link your WhatsApp account, why, and how long it is kept.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what is needed to link your WhatsApp to the bot.
  • Your phone number is not saved. Your linked session is kept encrypted for at most 5 minutes, or until you use the session ID once.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads or use tracking cookies.

What we collect and why

  • Your phone number (pairing code method only). It is sent to WhatsApp to request your pairing code. It is not stored. It may appear in a hidden or shortened form in the site’s technical log.
  • Your WhatsApp session. When you approve the link in WhatsApp, the linked session is created so the bot can act on your account. This is the most sensitive thing we handle, so it is stored encrypted and can be collected only with your one-time session ID.
  • Your session ID. It is made at random, works once and expires after 5 minutes. We also send it to your WhatsApp chat so you do not lose it.
  • Your internet address (IP). It is used only to limit repeated attempts and stop abuse. This is held in the site’s memory for a short time and is not written into a permanent record.
  • Attempts per phone number. To stop repeated attempts on one number, we keep a scrambled (hashed) fingerprint of it for a short time, not the number itself.

How long we keep it

  • Session: until the session ID is used, or 5 minutes after it was made, whichever comes first. Then it is removed from the site.
  • Attempt counters: about 15 minutes.
  • Phone number: not kept.

Once the bot has your session it keeps running on your account until you unlink it. See below.

Cookies and your device

The site sets no tracking or advertising cookies and uses no analytics or ad services. Your own device stores a few small settings: light or dark mode, sound, music, and, while you are in the middle of an attempt, a temporary note so a page refresh can continue it. You can clear these in your browser at any time.

Who else is involved

  • WhatsApp (Meta). Linking happens through WhatsApp, so WhatsApp receives your number and link request under its own privacy policy.
  • Hosting. The site runs on a cloud hosting provider, which handles the traffic and the files the site stores.

We do not sell or rent your information and do not share it for advertising.

Your choices

  • Unlink any time. In WhatsApp, open Settings, then Linked devices, and log out the TurboFex device. This cuts the bot’s access right away.
  • Keep your session ID private. Do not share it. If it is not used it expires on its own after 5 minutes.
  • Ask us. If you have a question or want help, contact us using the links below.

Children

The site is not meant for people who are too young to use WhatsApp where they live.

Changes

If we change how the site handles data, we will update this page and the date above.
