Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain words, what TurboFex Pair collects when you link your WhatsApp account, why, and how long it is kept.

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what is needed to link your WhatsApp to the bot.
  • Your full phone number is not saved. Your linked session is kept encrypted for at most 5 minutes, or until you use the session ID once.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads or use tracking cookies.

What we collect and why

  • Your phone number (pairing code method only). It is sent to WhatsApp to request your pairing code. It is not stored. It may appear in a hidden or shortened form in the site’s technical log.
  • Your WhatsApp session. When you approve the link in WhatsApp, the linked session is created so the bot can act on your account. This is the most sensitive thing we handle, so it is stored encrypted and can be collected only with your one-time session ID.
  • Your session ID. It is made at random, works once and expires after 5 minutes. We also send it to your WhatsApp chat so you do not lose it.
  • Your internet address (IP). It is used only to limit repeated attempts and stop abuse. This is held in the site’s memory for a short time and is not written into a permanent record.
  • Attempts per phone number. To stop repeated attempts on one number, we keep a scrambled (hashed) fingerprint of it for a short time, not the number itself.
  • A short activity log. So the site owners can see that the service works and spot abuse, each pairing attempt, and each visit to the site (counted at most once per 10 minutes for the same visitor, and not for search-engine or monitoring robots), is noted with the time, what happened (for example: code shown, linked, failed), your country, a shortened phone number (only the first and last digits show), a shortened internet address (the last part is hidden) and a short one-way fingerprint that cannot be turned back into the number or address. The log never contains your session, your session ID or your pairing code.
  • Blocks. If someone is blocked for abuse, only their one-way fingerprint is saved, with the reason and the date.
  • Developers and raters only. If you sign in with GitHub (in the Developer Centre or to rate a plugin), we keep your public GitHub number and username, when you last signed in, and the plugins you send in (the repository address, the code we copied, and the review result and note). If you rate a plugin, we also keep your rating and review, and they are shown publicly on the Plugins page together with your GitHub username (you can remove them yourself at any time). We do not ask for your email address or any permission on your GitHub account, and we do not keep the GitHub access token. A small cookie keeps you signed in for 7 days. This applies only to people who sign in with GitHub; people who just pair a phone are not affected. To have your developer record removed, contact us.

How long we keep it

  • Session: until the session ID is used, or 5 minutes after it was made, whichever comes first. Then it is removed from the site.
  • Attempt counters: about 15 minutes.
  • Activity log: 7 days, then it is deleted. A copy is kept in a private database service so it is not lost when the site restarts.
  • Phone number: not kept.

Once the bot has your session it keeps running on your account until you unlink it. See below.

Cookies and your device

The site sets no tracking or advertising cookies and uses no analytics or ad services. Your own device stores a few small settings: light or dark mode, sound effects, and, while you are in the middle of an attempt, a temporary note so a page refresh can continue it. You can clear these in your browser at any time.

Who can see this

A small number of site administrators can open a private dashboard that shows the activity log above, live counts, and the pairing sessions in progress. They sign in with a password and a second code, and everything they do there is recorded. The dashboard shows only shortened numbers and fingerprints. It never shows your full number, your session or your session ID.

Who else is involved

  • WhatsApp (Meta). Linking happens through WhatsApp, so WhatsApp receives your number and link request under its own privacy policy.
  • Hosting. The site runs on a cloud hosting provider, which handles the traffic and the files the site stores.
  • GitHub. Developers sign in through GitHub, and the site reads the plugin files you point it to from your public repository. GitHub handles that under its own privacy statement.
  • Database. The activity log, blocks and audit log (the shortened, scrambled data described above, never your session, session ID or full number) are copied to a private Supabase database, which only the site can access. Developer records (described above) are copied there too.

We do not sell or rent your information and do not share it for advertising.

Your choices

  • Unlink any time. In WhatsApp, open Settings, then Linked devices, and log out the TurboFex device. This cuts the bot’s access right away.
  • Keep your session ID private. Do not share it. If it is not used it expires on its own after 5 minutes.
  • Ask us. If you have a question or want help, contact us using the links below.

Children

The site is not meant for people who are too young to use WhatsApp where they live.

Changes

If we change how the site handles data, we will update this page and the date above.
